# Surface brief: Groww Beginner Mode app (groww-home.jsx)

Scope: whole app prototype (home, invest, detail pages, sheets, chat), token-level visual redesign only. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience: first-time Indian retail investors; Groww hiring panel views it in a phone frame on desktop.
Fixed: layout and flows, all copy, phone-in-browser frame. Craft bar chosen by user: Groww's current app.
Chosen direction: the category standard (canon), "Groww, played straight", picked by the user on the decision page (seed eaa5fdf4).

## Direction contract

THESIS: This is Groww's own app with Beginner Mode in it, so it must look like Groww shipped it: white surfaces, Groww green, quiet greys, one sans. It refuses the prototype's bespoke forest-and-mint world and any quirk Groww would not ship.
OWN-WORLD: Groww light tokens: white #FFFFFF cards on #F8F8F8 ground, hairlines #E9E9EB, ink #44475B, secondary #696C7C (Groww gray800, for AA), brand green #04B488 for fills, bars, dots and icons, green #00825C (Groww green11) for green text and white-label buttons, loss #D23A15 (red11), warning #8A5A00 on #FFF7DE, info #4452D6 on #F2F5FF. Figtree (closest open match to proprietary GrowwSans) for all text, tabular numerals. Radii 8px controls, 12px cards, pills for chips and segmented tabs only. Flat: borders, not shadows.
STORY: An evaluator sees a believable Groww screen at first glance, then notices the beginner guidance (next steps, explanations, guarded F&O) as the only new thing.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged layout. Header with avatar, greeting, AI, search, bell; NIFTY strip; portfolio as a white bordered card with ink value in large bold, invested and green gain below, green "View holdings" chip; allocation card; next steps. Bottom nav white with green active item.
FORM: canon (category standard), not on my grounded list; seed eaa5fdf4.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
